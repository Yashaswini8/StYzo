What is StyZo?

StyZo is a WhatsApp-based Host Growth Toolkit built for landlords and property owners. Everything you need to run a property happens right inside WhatsApp — pricing, listings and bookings.

💰

Pricing suggestions

Message a city and dates — "What's the price in Porto on July 10–12?" — and get a nightly rate suggestion in the local currency, with weekend and event bumps.

🏠

AI-drafted listings

Send property details or photos and StyZo's AI writes a catchy title and description ready to publish. Tweaks are a reply away.

📅

Booking management

Confirm or block dates right from the chat with simple messages like "Confirm July 15" or "Block August 5–7".
